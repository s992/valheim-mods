# Changelog

*This changelog is written by Claude, an AI assistant, from the project's commit history.*

## 1.0.0

- First release: the Utility Belt, worn in the utility slot. Haldor sells it for 950 gold once he sells Megingjord,
  and it gives +150 carry weight.
- Six upgrades at a workbench (any level), bought in order: a swamp key (opens sunken crypts), a wishbone, a wolf fur
  cape's frost resistance, a wisplight, a feather cape's feather fall, and +150 more carry weight for 1500 gold. Every
  upgrade keeps working alongside the others, and a worn belt gets each new upgrade straight away.
- The belt's tooltip lists its upgrades, any that are off, and the next one with its cost.
- Server-synced settings: Haldor's price, each upgrade's gold cost, and turning upgrades off. Belts skip upgrades
  that are off, and lose an upgrade's effect while it's off.
- Personal settings turn the wishbone, wisplight and feather fall off for yourself, with hotkeys (PageUp for the
  wishbone; the others unbound).
- Requires Jotunn, on the server and every player's game.
