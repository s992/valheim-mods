# Changelog

*This changelog is written by Claude, an AI assistant, from the project's commit history.*

## 1.0.0

- First release: the Thunderdome, a ward built with the hammer at a workbench (Misc tab). By default it costs 5
  Surtling Cores, 10 Fine Wood, 10 Ancient Bark and 10 Greydwarf Eyes.
- It raises a force field dome like the traders' camps have. Any hostile creature that comes inside, bosses included,
  is struck down by lightning and drops nothing. Players, tamed animals, passive animals and calm dvergr are left
  alone, and the lightning doesn't damage buildings.
- A Repel mode makes monsters turn away instead, the way they do at the traders' camps, and a Both mode turns them
  away and strikes any that come in anyway.
- The dome and a ring on the ground show the ward's reach while placing it. The dome stays visible on placed wards,
  and each player can hide it.
- Mode, radius (30 m by default, 5 to 100) and cost are set by the server in multiplayer, and only admins can change
  them. A new radius applies to wards already built. Every player needs the mod, along with Jotunn.
